Explore Our Exciting Trips

Ready for an adventure? Down the River Rafting Co. offers trips for all experience levels. Whether you're looking for a gentle float or an extreme adrenaline rush, we have the perfect trip for you! Have questions? Contact us today to book your experience.

Trip Types

Beginner

Perfect for families and those new to rafting. Gentle rapids and stunning canyon views make this the ideal introduction to white water rafting.

Intermediate

More excitement and challenge than beginner trips. Experience technical sections and moderate rapids with our experienced guides leading the way.

Advanced

For ultimate thrill-seekers! The most challenging rapids and pristine wilderness await. Only for experienced rafters seeking an unforgettable adventure.
